GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked

Is my pledge refundable if a studio doesn't open?

Yes. If a location fails to reach its funding goal within 18 months of your pledge, you receive a full refund. If the goal is reached and opening is delayed, you may choose a refund or a credit.

When will I receive my class packs?

Class packs are credited to your Barry's account 48 hours before the studio's official opening day. You'll receive an email with booking instructions as soon as the date is confirmed.

Can I pledge for multiple cities?

Absolutely. Each city has its own Founder campaign. You can pledge for one or all three — perks stack across all locations you support.

Is this an investment or a purchase?

For Supporter, Founding Member, and Elite tiers, this is a pre-purchase of products and experiences — not a financial investment. Studio Patron tier includes a conversation about equity participation, subject to legal and regulatory review.

Are classes in Hebrew or English?

Barry's Israel studios will offer classes in both Hebrew and English, with bilingual coaches. Our goal is a fully local experience with Barry's global standards.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, and bank transfer (העברה בנקאית). Payments are processed in ILS (Israeli Shekel).
